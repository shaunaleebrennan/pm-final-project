# Data-Backed Hypothesis, Module 3

- **Scenario:** My own initiative
- **Bet type:** Exploring the blue sky

## The hypothesis
> Based on only 30% of questionnaires getting every security question answered before the due date, and 42% submitted with a "to confirm" answer (hypothetical snapshot, to validate), we believe that solving the gap between a certification, region or policy change and the questionnaire answers it makes outdated for mid-market sales engineers answering buyer security questionnaires will result in sales engineers resolving flagged answers before the deadline instead of submitting "to confirm", so fewer outdated answers are signed into contracts (churn and legal risk) and fewer late-stage deals slip (revenue), as measured by a +27-point change (58% to 85%) in questionnaires submitted with no "to confirm" answers. We will protect median questionnaire turnaround (now 5.2 days, must not increase) and make a go/no-go decision after 8 weeks and at least 20 questionnaires, including one real posture change (scale at 75%+, iterate at 65 to 75%, kill below 65%).

## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** "Security took four days to get back to me. The questionnaire was due Friday, so I sent what I had and marked two answers 'to confirm'. I never went back to them." (H-05, hypothetical note to validate in interviews)
- **Quantitative (M3):** only 30% of questionnaires getting every security question answered before the due date, and 42% submitted with a "to confirm" answer (hypothetical snapshot, to validate)

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** mid-market sales engineers answering buyer security questionnaires
- **Goal:** Submit accurate questionnaire answers fast enough to keep the deal on schedule.
- **Friction:** They can't tell which answers a certification, region or policy change has made outdated, and security takes 3 to 4 days to confirm, so answers go out "to confirm".
- **Problem you are solving:** the gap between a certification, region or policy change and the questionnaire answers it makes outdated

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** sales engineers resolving flagged answers before the deadline instead of submitting "to confirm", so fewer outdated answers are signed into contracts (churn and legal risk) and fewer late-stage deals slip (revenue)
- **Primary success metric:** a +27-point change (58% to 85%) in questionnaires submitted with no "to confirm" answers
- **Guardrail metric:** median questionnaire turnaround (now 5.2 days, must not increase)
- **Decision window:** 8 weeks and at least 20 questionnaires, including one real posture change (scale at 75%+, iterate at 65 to 75%, kill below 65%)
