# Hypothesis & Success Metrics (Module 3)

## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** A sales engineer at a mid-market SaaS company who completes buyer security questionnaires against tight deal deadlines.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Submit accurate questionnaire answers fast enough to keep the deal on schedule.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** The AI tool fills gaps quickly, but they can't tell which answers came from a document that's a year old, so every AI-completed answer is trusted on faith (H-02). When they ask security, the reply takes three to four days, so answers go out marked "to confirm" (H-05).
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** Copy answers from the closest previous questionnaire, let an AI drafting tool fill the gaps, post uncertain questions in security's Slack channel and wait three to four days, then mark unresolved answers "to confirm" and submit. Nobody goes back to them.
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** We must solve the risk of signing enterprise deals on security answers that are no longer true by addressing sales engineers' reliance on copied, AI-finished answers and a security team that can't keep up with review.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** Check every drafted answer against current certifications, data residency and policies before submission, flag any answer a certification, region or policy change has made outdated or overstated, and send only those answers to security for review.

## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** "Security took four days to get back to me. The questionnaire was due Friday, so I sent what I had and marked two answers 'to confirm'. I never went back to them." (H-05, hypothetical note to validate in interviews)
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Only 30% of questionnaires get every security question answered before the due date, and 42% are submitted with at least one "to confirm" answer (hypothetical snapshot, to validate).
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** A mid-market sales engineer who wants to submit accurate security answers on deal time, but can't tell which answers a certification, region or policy change has made outdated, and can't get security to confirm them before the deadline.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Sales engineers can't see which answers a certification, region or policy change has made outdated, so they either wait days for security or submit unconfirmed answers.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Sales engineers send security only the flagged answers and resolve them before the deadline, instead of submitting "to confirm". That means fewer outdated answers signed into contracts (lower churn and legal risk) and fewer late-stage deals slipping a quarter (revenue).
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** % of questionnaires submitted with no "to confirm" answers: from 58% to 85%, a +27-point change.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Median questionnaire turnaround (now 5.2 days) must not increase. Adding a check can't make sales slower.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 8 weeks and at least 20 questionnaires, including at least one real certification, region or policy change. Scale at 75% or more, iterate between 65% and 75%, kill below 65% or if turnaround rises.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on H-05 and a hypothetical snapshot where only 30% of questionnaires get every security question answered in time and 42% go out with a "to confirm" answer, I believe that flagging answers made outdated by a certification, region or policy change for mid-market sales engineers will result in more questionnaires submitted with every high-risk answer confirmed, as measured by a +27-point change (58% to 85%) in questionnaires submitted with no "to confirm" answers. I will protect median questionnaire turnaround (5.2 days) and make a go/no-go decision after 8 weeks and 20 questionnaires.
