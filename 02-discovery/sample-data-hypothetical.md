# Sample data: security questionnaire answers (hypothetical)

> **Hypothetical sample set.** I wrote these from my own assumptions so I could practise the Module 2 synthesis method on my own initiative. They are not interviews or tickets from research I ran. Every item is an assumption to validate in real discovery interviews. Roles are generic; no real people or companies.

10 assumed research notes · 8 assumed process tickets · B2B security questionnaires, answer-accuracy focus

## Research notes (assumed)

H-01 · Sales engineer, mid-market SaaS "I start every questionnaire by finding the closest one we did before and pasting from it. Writing 250 answers from scratch isn't an option."

H-02 · Sales engineer "The AI tool fills the gaps fast, but I can't tell which answers it pulled from a document that's a year old."

H-03 · Security engineer (reviewer) "I get pinged in Slack for the same ten questions on five deals a week. Is EU residency live? Does SOC 2 cover the new product? I answer them all again."

H-04 · Proposal manager "Our answer library has over a thousand entries. Nobody owns keeping them current, so we trust the last-modified date."

H-05 · Sales engineer "Security took four days to get back to me. The questionnaire was due Friday, so I sent what I had and marked two answers 'to confirm'. I never went back to them."

H-06 · GRC lead "When we changed our data retention policy, I updated the policy document. I had no way to know which questionnaire answers still said the old number."

H-07 · Sales leader "Once legal has the contract, the security questionnaire is the slowest step. Deals slip a quarter waiting on it."

H-08 · Security engineer "We found an answer in a signed contract saying a product was covered by our ISO 27001 certificate. It was out of scope. That was a bad week."

H-09 · Sales engineer, new hire "I don't know which answers I'm allowed to change, so I don't change any."

H-10 · Aggregate (assumed) "Most answers are fine. The risky ones are a small set tied to certifications, regions and policies, but they look exactly like the rest."

## Process tickets (assumed)

T-01 · Sev: Critical A signed contract states a certification scope that excluded the product sold. Found in a customer audit, not before submission.

T-02 · Sev: High Security review requests posted in Slack take 3 to 4 days to answer. No queue, and no priority by deal stage or due date.

T-03 · Sev: High Answer library entries have no owner and no link to the policy, certificate or region they depend on.

T-04 · Sev: High The EU data residency answer says "available" in recent questionnaires while the option was still in beta.

T-05 · Sev: Medium The same 12 questions go to security across 5 concurrent deals. Answers given in Slack threads are not saved back to the library.

T-06 · Sev: Medium AI-drafted answers carry no citation to the source document or its date.

T-07 · Sev: Low Buyer questionnaire formats vary (spreadsheet, portal, PDF); copy-paste breaks formatting.

T-08 · Sev: Low The public trust centre still lists a sub-processor removed last quarter.
