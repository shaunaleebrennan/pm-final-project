# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** My own initiative

## Persona
- **Role:** A sales engineer at a mid-market SaaS company who completes buyer security questionnaires against tight deal deadlines.
- **Goal:** Submit accurate questionnaire answers fast enough to keep the deal on schedule.
- **Friction:** The AI tool fills gaps quickly, but they can't tell which answers came from a document that's a year old, so every AI-completed answer has to be trusted on faith (H-02).

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** An AI drafting tool, the security team's Slack channel, files of previous questionnaires, and the answer library's last-modified dates. The sample research doesn't name specific products. The named alternatives a buyer could choose are AI answer tools (Loopio, Responsive, Conveyor) and public trust centres. My journey map sets out how the check differs and the risk that one of them adds it.
- **The process:** 1) Copy answers from the closest previous questionnaire (H-01). 2) Run the AI tool to fill the gaps and accept its answers on trust, with only last-modified dates as a freshness signal (H-02, H-04). 3) Post uncertain questions in security's Slack channel and wait three to four days (T-02). 4) At the deadline, mark unresolved answers "to confirm" and submit (H-05). 5) Never go back to them, so the wording carries into the next questionnaire (H-05, H-09).
- **Core frustration:** The check is slower than the deal. A three-to-four-day Slack wait against a Friday deadline forces a choice between accuracy and submission, and the workaround chooses submission.
- **The evidence:** Hypothetical note H-05, to validate in interviews: "Security took four days to get back to me. The questionnaire was due Friday, so I sent what I had and marked two answers 'to confirm'. I never went back to them." The cost shows in T-01: a wrong certification scope was found in a customer audit, after the contract was signed.

## Future-state journey map
1. Prepare · opens questionnaire, sees matched answers with owners → starts from answers tied to live sources · copied answers carry no provenance (H-01, H-02, T-03)
2. Draft · reads each AI draft beside its source and date → judges freshness without trusting the tool blindly · no source age (H-02, T-06)
3. Verify · sends only flagged answers to security, with the changed control → a decision, not a Slack question · 3 to 4 day waits, repeat questions (T-02, T-05)
4. Submit and learn · resolves every flag, saves security decisions to the library → nothing goes out "to confirm" · unchecked answers signed (H-05, T-05, T-01)

## Competitive advantages
1) Flags affected answers when the change happens, not after a customer audit finds them (T-01). 2) Sends security only flagged answers, cutting repeat questions across concurrent deals (T-05). 3) Gives every answer an owner, source and date, replacing trust on faith with a check (H-02, T-03).
